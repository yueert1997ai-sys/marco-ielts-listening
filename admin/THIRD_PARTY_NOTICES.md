## ECDICT

The generated `public/data/ecdict-lite.json` contains a filtered and compacted
subset of [ECDICT](https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT), a free English to
Chinese dictionary database distributed under the MIT License.

Copyright and license terms remain with the ECDICT contributors.

## WebLLM and Qwen

The optional local fallback loads WebLLM and a compatible Qwen model in the
user's browser on demand. Model assets are not bundled in this repository.
Their upstream licenses and notices apply.
